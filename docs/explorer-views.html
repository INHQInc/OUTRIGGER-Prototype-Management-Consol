What changes vs today

Today's tile carries a paragraph of copy and two competing CTAs — Book Now and Learn More. Guests click neither (+1 and −5.9%). Here: one line of character, two attribute chips, one action. The dashed rate slot is where a from-rate would sit only if the SynXis best-price widget can supply it — the card works without it.

The honest caveat on this one

Property titles rose +11.3% while Learn More fell and Book Now stayed flat — guests are opening properties to compare them, which is what this view is for. But the rate column is empty without the best-price widget, and the booking engine's own “Select a Hotel” page already does rated, sortable comparison. This view has to earn its place on the attributes the engine doesn't carry: setting, known-for, best-for. If it can't, send people to the engine's list instead.

Why this one is the keeper

SynXis has no geography. A first-time visitor does not know the islands are separate flights, that all four O‘ahu properties sit on one stretch of Waikīkī, or how far Maui is from Kona. This view answers a question guests don't know they have — and it is the only one of the three that a booking engine could never replace. Your Regional Map Display prototype is most of this already; what it needs is the exit changed from “Visit property” to a dated rates link.

What I'd decide from here

Three questions, in the order they matter

  • Does the capture bar belong in the hero or above the explorer? In the hero it catches everyone; above the explorer it catches only the engaged. The hero is the stronger position and the bigger design argument — it's also where v1 proved attention exists.
  • Does the list view survive? It only earns its place on setting / known-for / best-for — attributes the engine doesn't carry. If we can't populate those well, the engine's own list is better than ours and we should send people to it.
  • Is the map a view, or the whole thing? There's a real argument that the map is the explorer — pick an island, see what's there, go — and that grid and list are just the map's results in other shapes.

Once those are settled, the build is: one capture bar, one result set, one exit URL pattern, and whichever views survive.
